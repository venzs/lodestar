# World of Warcraft: Forever — what is verifiable (as of 2026-09-18)

Confidence tags used below:

- **[SOURCE]** read directly from Blizzard's Forever UI export (build 1.60.1.69893, `AllowLoadGameType camelot` files) or from the generated API docs in `tools/wow-api/api.json`. Highest confidence: this is what the client actually does.
- **[BLIZZ]** Blizzard news post, CM forum post, or a developer quoted by name in an interview.
- **[PRESS]** press / fan-site write-up of a BlizzCon panel or hands-on (Icy Veins, Blizzard Watch, Warcraft Tavern). Usually right, occasionally garbled.
- **[DATAMINE]** fan-site tables built from the beta client (classicwow.gg says "captured from build 1.60.1.69800"). Numbers may change.
- **[PROBE]** our own observation on the beta (`/lode probe`).

Dates: BlizzCon reveal 2026-09-12, beta 2026-09-17 → 2026-10-21 (last full day), launch 2026-11-04 15:00 PST. [BLIZZ]

## 2. Legacy system

Blizzard's description [BLIZZ] <https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303313/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-panel-recap>: points are earned by "completing Legacy Challenges across several categories", "shared across all alts, while each character spends those points separately in their own trees"; at launch "up to 16 points" can be spent per character and "up to 65 Legacy Points" can be earned; overflow feeds a cosmetic reward track (Spectral Bear Cub etc.); Hardcore is isolated (progress flows out of Hardcore, never in).

How it is built (this is what an addon can read) [SOURCE] `Blizzard_LegacySystem/*`, `Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/LegacyConstantsDocumentation.lua`:

- Three pages: **Reward Track**, **Challenges**, **Tree** (`LegacySystemFrame`, toggled by `ToggleLegacySystemUI()`, binding `TOGGLELEGACYSYSTEM`, micro button `LegacyMicroButton`).
- Trees are **C_Traits trees**: `Constants.LegacyConsts.LEGACY_TREE_PROFESSIONS_ID = 1187`, `LEGACY_TREE_ADVENTURE_ID = 1188`, `LEGACY_TREE_PROGRESSION_ID = 1189` (the third is labelled `LEGACY_TREE_PROGRESSION` in code; marketing/Icy Veins call it **Resourcefulness**). Points are trait currency `LEGACY_POINTS_TRAIT_CURRENCY_ID = 4225`; read with `C_Traits.GetConfigIDByTreeID(treeID)` then `C_Traits.GetTreeCurrencyInfo(configID, treeID, true)[1]` → `{quantity, maxQuantity, spent, spentInTree}`. Nodes via `C_Traits.GetTreeNodes`/`GetNodeInfo`; commit/undo/reset use the normal talent-frame mixin (`C_Traits.ResetTree`, `ConfigHasStagedChanges`). Events: `TRAIT_TREE_CURRENCY_INFO_UPDATED`, `TRAIT_NODE_CHANGED`, `TRAIT_CONFIG_UPDATED`.
- The reward track is a **Major Faction renown track**: `LEGACY_REWARD_TRACK_FACTION_ID = 2802`; `C_MajorFactions.GetCurrentRenownLevel(2802)` is the "points" number shown; levels/rewards via `C_MajorFactions.GetRenownLevels` / `GetRenownRewardsForLevel`; event `MAJOR_FACTION_RENOWN_LEVEL_CHANGED`. The micro button is **disabled until renown level > 0** (`LegacyMicroButtonMixin:IsLegacySystemUnlocked`), i.e. until the first challenge is done — consistent with the known-issues note about level 25.
- Challenges are **achievements**: the Challenges page uses `GetCategoryList`, `GetCategoryNumAchievements`, `GetAchievementInfo`, `CRITERIA_UPDATE`, `ACHIEVEMENT_EARNED`, and a per-character saved variable `LegacyChallengesUnviewed`. There is no achievement micro button or `TOGGLEACHIEVEMENT` binding on camelot — the Legacy UI is the achievement UI. The camelot micro menu is: Character, Professions, Spellbook, Talents, **Legacy**, Quest Log, **Housing** (gated by `Enum.GameRule.HousingDashboardDisabled`; Blizzard's reveal post mentions housing as a future update), Guild, Group Finder (`C_LFGInfo.IsGroupFinderEnabled`, toggled by `LFG_ENABLED_STATE_CHANGED`), Collections, Encounter Journal, Help, Store, Main Menu (`Blizzard_MicroMenu/Camelot/MicroMenuContainerOverrides.lua`).
- Node `LEGACY_TREE_ADVENTURE_TALENTED_NODE_ID = 110298` ("Talented") lowers `MIN_TALENT_LEVEL` by its rank (`PlayerSpellsMicroButtonMixin:GetTalentUnlockLevel`, camelot override). This cross-checks the fan-site perk lists below.

Perk lists (names/effects) [DATAMINE] <https://classicwow.gg/forever/guides/legacy-tree>, [PRESS] <https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/legacy-system>:
Adventure — Well Rested (5, +4 %/rank rested XP rate and cap), Talented (5, talent points from level 9→5), High Alert (2, stealth detection), Thrill of Adventure (5, 1 % HP/mana per non-trivial kill, not in instances/BGs), Field Guide (3, −8 % camp-feature cooldown), Frequent Flier (1, −50 % flight cost, +20 % flight speed; needs 10 pts in tree), Field Medicine (2, −5 s Recently Bandaged).
Professions — Master Chef (5), Working Overtime (5, +4 % skill-up chance), Bartering (2, −5 % vendor prices), Dedicated Study (1, +1 lowest tradeskill, 23 h CD), Bountiful Harvest (5, +20 % "Scarce" gathering mats), Performance Bonus (3, Merchant's Favor), Luremaster (2).
Resourcefulness — For Great Honor (5), Gourmand (3, +33 % food-buff duration), Permanence (2, +50 % party/raid buff and camp-rest duration), The Quick and the Dead (2), Reagent Economy (1, no vendor reagents; tier-1 camp objects free), Reinforce (5, −8 % durability loss on death), Diplomat (5, +2 % rep).
Challenge categories seen: Classes (level 25/45/60), Tradeskills (150/225/300), Adventure, Dungeons, Raids, PvP. Reward-track thresholds reported as 15/25/40/55 points (Ironforge Air Rifle, Spectral Bear Cub, tabard, Spectral Bear mount) [PRESS Icy Veins].
Blizzard Watch first look (screens of all three panels): <https://blizzardwatch.com/2026/09/12/first-look-wow-forevers-legacy-system/> [PRESS].

**"Adventuring" is not a profession or secondary skill.** The profession book on camelot lists exactly prof1, prof2, First Aid, Fishing, Cooking (`GetProfessions()` → `prof1, prof2, faid, fish, cook` in `Blizzard_ProfessionsBook/Camelot`). "Adventure" is the Legacy tree 1188 and a Legacy Challenge category. [SOURCE]

## 3. Camping and campfires

Blizzard [BLIZZ] deep-dive recap: the Cooking Fire becomes a **Basic Campfire** where players "rest and gain access to useful benefits such as vendors, repairs, profession workspaces, and one-hour buffs"; a campfire takes "up to three special crafted objects", "each player can contribute one object", objects share a "one-hour cooldown"; "every profession has three different objects learned at different skill levels" (Blacksmith Sharpening Wheel = attack power, Tailor Faction Banner = spirit, Herbalist Incense Candle = intellect, Alchemist Alchemy Lab, Leatherworker Tanning Rack, Cook upgraded campfires holding 5 or 10 objects; advanced ones are "Blueprint recipes from dungeon bosses").

Beta-build details [DATAMINE] <https://classicwow.gg/forever/guides/camping> (updated 09-18): Basic Campfire Kit is a Cooking recipe at skill 1 (Simple Wood + Flint and Tinder); Journeyman kit at Cooking 140 (5 slots), Expert at 220 (10 slots); the site lists a **5-minute** kit cooldown (Blizzard says one hour for the buffs/objects — treat the 5 min as unconfirmed). First-tier objects at skill 20: Blacksmithing Sharpening Wheel (Strength, does not stack with Strength of Earth), Alchemy Mana Well (mana regen while sitting, vs Blessing of Wisdom), Tailoring Faction Banner (Spirit), Leatherworking Camp Tent (rested XP), Herbalism Incense Candle (Intellect, vs Arcane Intellect), Mining Lodestone (melee AP, vs Blessing of Might), Skinning Camp Chair (2 % crit), Enchanting Enchanted Lute (armor/all stats/resists, vs Mark of the Wild), Engineering Reagent Bot (reagent vendor), First Aid Kit (Stamina, vs Fortitude), Fishing Fish Bowl (8 % stats, vs Kings). Buffs last one hour and are obtained by sitting/crafting at the fire for about a minute; camp buffs are "variants of, and do not stack with" the class buffs named. Icy Veins (09-15) agrees on the shape and lists Blacksmithing Anvil (140) and Master Forge (300) as tiers 2/3 [PRESS] <https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/camping>.

Client side [SOURCE]: there is **no camping namespace, event or UI addon** in the export (`PLAYER_CAMPING` is the old logout event; `Enum.GameRule.DisableCampsites` is the character-select warband camp). Campfires are game objects + spells + auras; an addon sees them only through `C_UnitAuras` (buff names/spell IDs, unknown until harvested), `IsResting()`/`PLAYER_UPDATE_RESTING` (if a camp grants rested state — unverified), `C_TradeSkillUI.IsNearProfessionSpellFocus` (workspace checks), and merchant/repair events at camp vendors.

## 4. Professions, cooking, food XP

- "Over 600 new recipes across professions and level ranges"; "crafted food that can grant a small experience bonus and stat boosts"; food buffs "received a major update". [BLIZZ] deep-dive recap.
- BlizzCon example: eat 10 s → 15-min Well Fed with a stat line and "5 % increased experience from kills". [PRESS] Icy Veins camping guide; [DATAMINE] classicwow.gg.
- Merchant's Favor is a currency from the Azeroth Commerce Authority / Durotar Supply and Logistics factions (SoD's Waylaid Supplies); buys tabards, supply packs, a Dwarven Tradeskill Sign toy. [PRESS] <https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/waylaid-supplies-factions-from-sod-spotted-in-wow-forever/>
- Fishing: tackle boxes, lava fishing, dailies in Blackmaw Village; First Aid bandages heal more. [PRESS/DATAMINE] <https://www.zockify.com/forever/professions/>
- Client: the retail `C_TradeSkillUI` (89 functions: recipes, schematics, `GetRecipesTracked`, `IsRecipeProfessionLearned`, `GetProfessionInfoBySkillLineID`) and the Mainline professions frame with camelot overrides; `TRACKED_RECIPE_UPDATE`, `NEW_RECIPE_LEARNED`, `SKILL_LINES_CHANGED` events. Weapon skills and Defense are real skill lines (`C_SkillInfo`, IDs 43/44/45/46/54/55/136/160/162/172/173/176/226/228/229, Feral 3014, Defense 95) and the camelot Skills tab computes hit/crit/glancing vs +0/+3 targets from them. [SOURCE] `Blizzard_UIPanels_Game/Camelot/SkillsFrame.lua`, `PaperDollFrameStats.lua`

## 5. Classes, races, combat rules

- New combos: Gnome Priest, Human Hunter, Dwarf Shaman, Orc Mage, Troll Warlock, Undead Paladin, "more planned". [BLIZZ] deep-dive recap. Skyborne (new elf race, Zephras Isle start, either faction) — Alliance: Warrior/Hunter/Mage/Rogue/Druid; Horde: Warrior/Hunter/Rogue/Druid/Shaman [PRESS] <https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/guides/wow-forever-race-class-combinations>. `CLASS_SORT_ORDER` on camelot is the nine vanilla classes. [SOURCE]
- Starting/class quests for the new combos: **nothing published**; Blizzard's recap only describes the Paladin rework (Judgement no longer consumes Seals; "Twist of Light supports Seal Twisting without requiring a swing-timer add-on"). Capture them with `/lode record` on the beta.
- Combat: mob TTK "roughly 10–15 seconds"; "Spell, melee, and ranged hit chance are combined", same for crit; "Bonus healing now also includes one third as much bonus damage"; new stats that reduce enemy parry/dodge chance (i.e. expertise-like); caster weapons give spell damage/healing. [BLIZZ] recap. The camelot paper doll still exposes the three hit values and three crit values separately (`GetHitModifier`/`GetRangedHitModifier`/`GetSpellHitModifier` + `GetCombatRatingBonus(CR_HIT_*)`; `GetCritChance`/`GetRangedCritChance`/`GetSpellCritChance`) and only shows the max. [SOURCE]
- Talents: `Blizzard_PlayerSpells/Camelot/ClassTalents` is the C_Traits-based frame with **dual-spec tabs** (`GetNumSpecGroups() > 1` → second tab enabled, `C_SpecializationInfo.SetActiveSpecGroup`). Whether dual spec is purchasable on the beta is unverified. [SOURCE] Fan sites count "111 new talents / 284 changed" [DATAMINE] <https://wowforevertalents.com/what-to-expect/>.
- No one-button rotations; no Shaman tanks at launch (Tim Jones). [BLIZZ] Kotaku.

## 6. Levels, zones, quests, dungeons, raids, PvP

- Level cap 60 at launch. Beta: cap 20 at start, "raised to Level 30 after a couple of weeks" (Clay Stone / Nora Mills via Destin, Icy Veins 09-14); week-1 content: Zephras Isle, Hall of Thanes (13–18), Ruins of Lordaeron (15–20), "all zone content up to level 20"; more zones, dungeons and the new battleground open later; a "Server Slam" during the test; beta progress is wiped. [BLIZZ] beta post; [PRESS] <https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/the-wow-forever-beta-starts-at-level-20-level-30-after-couple-of-weeks/>
- New zones: Zephras Isle (1–12), Riverglades (Eastern Kingdoms, ~30–45, "over 150 new quests", goblin port), Shen'Dralas (between Desolace and Mulgore, ~30–50), Mount Hyjal (post-Archimonde, endgame), Dalaran (city + dungeon). "Over 1,000 new quests" overall, old zones expanded. [PRESS] <https://blizzardwatch.com/2026/09/13/know-world-warcraft-forevers-new-zones-quests-dungeons-raids/>, <https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/all-new-zones-areas-in-wow-forever-revealed-at-blizzcon/> (level ranges differ between sites; treat as approximate).
- Nine new dungeons at launch: Hall of Thanes 13–18, Ruins of Lordaeron 15–20, Excavation Site 24–29, City of Dalaran 28–33, Drowned City 35–40, Krol'dok Stronghold 40–45 (WT says 40–55), Alcaz Island Prison 48–53, Blackmaw Hold 55–60, Shaper's Terrace 58–60. No level scaling. [PRESS] Blizzard Watch.
- Dungeon XP: trash XP "significantly reduced", dungeon quests give "substantially more"; total 1–60 XP unchanged (Zierhut/Nuthals via The Sun, reported by guided.news — no exact numbers). [PRESS] <https://guided.news/en/news/wow-forever-is-deliberately-nerfing-dungeon-grinding-but-quests-will-reward-significantly-more-xp/>
- Meeting stones open an LFG window but **do not summon** ("Warlocks remain necessary" — Nuthals). Groups must share a ruleset; no cross-faction grouping. [BLIZZ] Kotaku; [PRESS] wowforevertalents.
- Raids: Barrow Deeps (10) and Hyjal Summit (20) on 2026-12-09; two more in spring 2027; a revamped classic raid + a new one in summer 2027; every tier has 10- and 20-player options, sometimes 40 (Nuthals). [PRESS] Blizzard Watch; [BLIZZ] Kotaku.
- PvP: honor → "rank 14 within that patch cycle", cosmetic rewards + statted PvP armor (Jones). New 15v15 battleground Darkspear Island. Client: PvP rank is a Major Faction renown track, `PVP_RANK_POINTS_FACTION_ID = 2800`, seasons via `C_SeasonInfo.GetTimeUntilCurrentPVPSeasonEnd`, `Enum.PvPRanks`. [BLIZZ] Kotaku; [SOURCE] `Camelot/PVPRankFrame.lua`
- Mounts: "we've readjusted the gold cost to not be on the mount itself, but on the training" (Jones); no flying, three new ship routes. Riding levels not stated. [BLIZZ] Kotaku; [PRESS]
- Rulesets instead of realms: Normal, PvP, Roleplaying, Hardcore (Hardcore "after launch … later in the winter"); two-part character names unique per region; collections and Legacy points account-wide except Hardcore. Client has `C_GameRules.IsHardcoreActive()`, `IsSelfFoundAllowed()`, hardcore death/guild-handoff popups. [BLIZZ] recap; [SOURCE] `Blizzard_Game/Camelot/EventImplementation.lua`
- Transmog exists with a "Classic Mode" opt-out; uncommon/rare dungeon drops teach appearance to all eligible looters. Collections journal (mounts, pets, toys, wardrobe, heirlooms) is present (`TOGGLECOLLECTIONS*` bindings). [BLIZZ] recap; [SOURCE] `Bindings_Camelot.xml`
- Day/night: the camelot minimap has a day/night dial driven by `DIEL_CYCLE_CHANGED` and `C_DateAndTime.IsDayTime()`. Gameplay meaning unknown. [SOURCE] `Blizzard_Minimap/Camelot/Diel.lua`
- Quest UI: quest-type shown as text not icons; objective tracker has no timer bars; trivial-quest minimap filter exposed in Settings. Our probe found **no quest POIs for old-world quests** on the beta (`Lodestar_Guide/Data.lua` comment). [SOURCE]; [PROBE]
